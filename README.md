# BeaverSpot

## What is BeaverSpot?

BeaverSpot is a web app built to solve a problem every CCNY student knows: finding a good place on campus to study, work, or just relax between classes. Instead of wandering around hoping to stumble on an open outlet or a quiet corner, students can open BeaverSpot, say what they need, and instantly get recommendations for the best spot on campus.

Whether it's a quiet room to cram before an exam, a group-friendly space to work on a project, or just somewhere with a microwave and a working outlet, BeaverSpot points students in the right direction regarding campus spaces.

## Features

### 1. Interactive Campus Map & Building Explorer
Browse an interactive, clickable map of CCNY buildings and explore study spaces floor by floor. Each space is pinned with quick-glance icons showing its key amenities, so students can see what's available at a glance before they even walk over.

### 2. Smart Recommendation Engine
Tell BeaverSpot what you need — "a quiet spot for 2 hours before my 2pm class" — and it ranks the best available spaces for you based on noise level, walk time, and amenities. 

### 3. Amenity & Attribute Database
Every space in BeaverSpot is backed by detailed, structured data: outlets, microwaves, wifi strength, noise level, natural light, operating hours, and capacity. This database is the foundation that powers the map and the recommendation engine.

### 4. User Feedback & Ratings
After using a space, students can rate their experience — comfort, accuracy of the noise level, overall vibe. Over time, this builds a reputation score for each room and helps surface the most-loved study spots on campus.
